Checked 2026-10-25. Line numbers come from copies of the four files taken at 08:15 CDT. Section 5.10 of the user research doc was rewritten while this check ran (file saved 08:12), so earlier line numbers no longer apply.

Short answer on "Stanford 70%": the only 70% adoption figure tied to Stanford in these files is ledger row V5-11 (F:740). It is not used in the text. The 70% comes from the Stanford AI Index 2026 web summary ("Generative AI is now used in at least one business function at 70% of organizations"). The chapter PDF from the same report says 79% in its text and chart, and both cite McKinsey's 2025 survey. So the 70% is not Stanford's own measure. It is a McKinsey figure, and the web summary appears to misstate it. The correct relayed figure is 79% for generative AI, or 88% for any AI. The other Stanford-linked 70% figures in W are model accuracy or user-preference numbers, not adoption.
