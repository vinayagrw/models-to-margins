# V8. Gartner 71% and 21% fact check

Checked 2026-10-25 by a fresh fact checker.

## Claims under test

1. "71% of organizations expect to deploy agentic AI within the next 12 months"
2. "only 21% currently have agentic AI governance capabilities"

The two files in this project disagreed on the survey behind them. One said a Q2 2026 survey of more than 2,000 CIOs and technology executives. The other said a Q3 survey of IT risk leaders.

## Verdict

| Figure | Verdict | Survey base |
|---|---|---|
| 71% expect to deploy agentic AI within 12 months | Verified | Q2 2026 Gartner survey of over 2,000 CIOs and technology executives |
| 21% have agentic AI governance capabilities | Verified | Same Q2 2026 survey |

The survey base is settled. Both figures come from the Q2 2026 Gartner survey of over 2,000 CIOs and technology executives. The Q3 2026 survey of 303 CIOs and IT risk management leaders is a different survey, and the release uses it only for the 25% risk alignment figure. The "Q3 IT risk leaders" label in our other file came from the Techstrong.ai article, which places the 71% and 21% in the same paragraph as the Q3 survey without naming a base for them.

Best URL: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-10-19-gartner-it-symposium-xpo-keynote-release-2026

Best open copy for readers whose tools get a 403 from gartner.com: https://finviz.com/news/404098/gartner-says-ai-risk-is-rising-faster-than-ai-value
