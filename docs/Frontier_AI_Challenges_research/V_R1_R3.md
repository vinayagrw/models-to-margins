## Flycatcher: who pays

The lawyer pays personally. The National Law Review write-up says the order shifted the cost onto the offending lawyer personally, a solo practitioner, payable within 30 days. The CourtListener docket for 1:24-cv-09429 shows the February opinion sanctioning Mr. Feldman (counsel for defendant Affable Avenue) by default judgment against his client, and ordering Mr. MacMull (counsel for co-defendant Top Experience) to file a fee application as to Mr. Feldman. So "a lawyer paid about $39K" is right and "the offending side" in R3 should be changed to "the offending lawyer personally".
