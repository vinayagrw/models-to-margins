## Spec checks on the section 8 sequence diagrams

- Pattern 1, token. Order is right for human present checkout. The merchant charges through its own PSP, not straight to the network, and an SPT can stop in requires_action for 3D Secure. Row 20.
- Pattern 2, mandate. Order matches the AP2 v0.2 human not present flow: user signed open mandate with the agent key as cnf, merchant signed checkout JWT, agent signed closed mandates, credential from the credential provider, merchant charges through its processor, receipts. The note over the diagram credits the user with signing the cart, which the agent key does. Row 34.
- Pattern 3, HTTP 402. x402 v2 order is 402 with PAYMENT-REQUIRED, retry with PAYMENT-SIGNATURE, verify, serve, settle, 200 with PAYMENT-RESPONSE. The diagram settles before serving. Row 50.
- Pattern 4, registered agent. Matches Web Bot Auth, except the IETF draft requires created and expires but leaves the nonce to deployment policy. The draft recommends at most 24 hours validity and names no mandatory algorithm. Ed25519 is in the examples and in the Cloudflare TAP note. Row 63.

## Decision tree by use case

- Consumer checkout: sensible. Present plus merchant of record gives registered agent plus token, which fits UCP and the ChatGPT browser path.
- Consumer, human away: leaf T4 pays with no mandate, against verdict step 3 and ADR-3. Rows 66 and 67.
- B2B procurement: weak. T5 names no credential, B2B on card drops the human set spend limits that requirement 1.1.2 asks for, SCA is never asked, and UCC 4A does not clearly reach stablecoin rails. Rows 68 and 69.
- Machine pay per call: sensible. Under the floor gives 402 from a prepaid budget, above it gives 402 with a card token through MPP. The x402 upto and batch-settlement schemes are not used.
- Payouts: sensible. Linked out, and AP2 and ACP stay Spec there.

## Gaps

- Section 1. Gap: No requirement to sign outbound order webhooks and reject replays. Why it matters: UCP says business to platform webhooks MUST be signed and ACP 2026-04-17 added webhook signing with replay protection. A gateway that skips it fails both specs. Research question: What signing method, key publication and replay window do UCP and ACP require for order webhooks?
- Section 1. Gap: The 300 ms p95 target ignores step up authentication. Why it matters: An SPT can stop in requires_action for 3D Secure, which adds seconds and a human. Research question: How often do agent token payments trigger 3D Secure or passkey step up, and what latency do they add?
- Section 3. Gap: The thesis says credentials converge, yet ACP still accepts fpan. Why it matters: Raw card numbers can still pass through delegate payment into a PSP vault, which affects the PCI claim in 1.2. Research question: What share of ACP delegate payment calls send fpan rather than network tokens, and which PSPs accept fpan?
- Section 5. Gap: No rule for which identity wins when UCP-Agent and Signature-Agent disagree. Why it matters: UCP makes UCP-Agent lookup mandatory and Signature-Agent optional, and treats the tag as a hint, so trust tiers could differ by verifier. Research question: How do UCP verifiers resolve identity when UCP-Agent and Signature-Agent point at different keys?
- Section 5. Gap: ADR-5 says nothing about refunds on the 402 lane. Why it matters: Coinbase advises a transfer back and Stripe offers refunds on machine payments it settles, so the refund promise can be built in. Research question: What refund mechanisms do x402 facilitators and Stripe machine payments offer, at what cost and within what window?
- Section 8. Gap: No B2B leaf carries buyer approval limits on card rails. Why it matters: Requirement 1.1.2 asks for human set spend limits and the Visa BILL pilot pays only buyer approved invoices. Research question: How do the Visa, BILL and Billtrust pilot and Mastercard Agent Pay carry buyer approval limits into the token or the payment?
- Section 8. Gap: x402 upto and batch-settlement schemes are absent from the patterns and the tree. Why it matters: upto fits metered calls and batch-settlement fits sub cent calls, and AWS AgentCore already uses upto. Research question: Which facilitators support x402 upto and batch-settlement in production, with what fees and refund handling?
- Section 8. Gap: UCC 4A is applied to all final rails. Why it matters: UCC 4A covers wires and funds transfers, not clearly stablecoin transfers, so the B2B final rail leaf may rest on the wrong law. Research question: Which law governs a business's agent initiated stablecoin payment in the US, UCC 4A, Article 12 or contract, and what security procedure counts?
- Section 8. Gap: SCA for human present agent checkout in the EU. Why it matters: The tree asks about SCA only when the human is away. Research question: Has the EBA or any EU issuer said how SCA applies when an agent completes a human present checkout?
- Section 8. Gap: VIC live status is unproven. Why it matters: The tree leaf and the token pattern lean on VIC, but the developer page does not say live or GA. Research question: Is Visa Intelligent Commerce agentic token provisioning in production, in which markets, and with how many enablers?
