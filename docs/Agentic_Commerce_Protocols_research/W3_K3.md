Sections 7.1, 7.2, 7.3 (doc lines 570 to 622). Statements checked: 39. Keep: 24. Fix: 15. Cut: 0. Not checked: 15 statements. Pages opened: 45 (4 refused with 403 or 429, 1 certificate error). Checked on 2026-11-15.

Most serious problems
1. The doc says no network rule allocates the loss. Visa's public rules (edition 2026-10-24, agentic text last updated 2026-04) add section 4.1.23 Agentic Platform Requirements. Rule 4.1.23.10 makes the cardholder responsible for an enrolled agent's actions as if the cardholder initiated the transaction, and 4.1.23.3 makes the agent obtain the cardholder's acknowledgement of that. This touches 7.1, the card row in 7.2, the Visa row and plain reading in 7.3, and page item challenges[0] ("No law, network rule or court case...").
2. The EU AI Act row is stale. The Digital Omnibus on AI is law: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, adopted 2026-07-08, Official Journal 2026-07-24, in force 2026-07-27. Annex III high risk duties move to 2027-12-02 and product embedded ones to 2028-08-02. The doc still says Council approval was pending.
3. The PSD3 and PSR status is incomplete. The Council adopted its first reading position in late October 2026 (sources give 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29). The Parliament's final vote is planned for 2026-12-14 and Official Journal publication is expected in 2027-01. The PSR would apply about 21 months later, near 2028-11.

## Gaps

Section 7.1
- Gap: Visa's agentic rules (4.1.23) are absent from the leader summary. Why it matters: a client told "no network rule" will misprice cardholder side exposure and expect fraud claims to work on Visa agent payments. Research question: For Visa Agentic Transactions, do issuers decline fraud disputes under condition 10.4 by citing rule 4.1.23.10, and how does that sit with Reg E 1005.2(m) and Reg Z 1026.12(b)?
- Gap: Stripe offers agent purchase protection on Link for US purchases (Payments Dive 2026-10-28), not mentioned beside Amex. Why it matters: PSP level protection changes who absorbs wrong purchases on that route. Research question: What do Stripe's Link terms say about agent purchase protection (scope, caps, user monitoring duties), and do other wallets or PSPs offer one?

Section 7.2
- Gap: The CMA page update of 2026-10-30 added a DRCF joint statement on agentic AI. Why it matters: a joint UK regulator view on agents may bear on accountability for consumer side agents. Research question: What does the DRCF joint statement on agentic AI say about accountability and consumer protection for AI agents, including payments?
- Gap: UK reg 77(4)(c) and PSD2 article 74(2) put the loss on the PSP when SCA was required but not applied. Why it matters: agent payments run under SCA exemptions or merchant initiated mandates may shift unauthorised loss to PSPs, which changes the "who carries the loss" column for the UK and EU. Research question: When an agent payment goes through without SCA, who bears an unauthorised loss under PSRs 2017 reg 77(4)(c) and PSD2 article 74(2), and has the FOS ruled on delegated credentials?
- Gap: Mastercard rule text is still unread while Visa now has explicit agent rules. Why it matters: a client may assume both networks treat agents alike. Research question: Do the 2026 Mastercard Rules, Transaction Processing Rules or Chargeback Guide define agentic transactions or make the cardholder responsible for agent actions?
- Gap: India agentic UPI pilots (for example Razorpay with NPCI, reported 2026-02, not opened) may already be live. Why it matters: "Not set" may hide live liability terms. Research question: Which agentic UPI payments are live in India by 2026-11, under which NPCI circulars, and with what liability split?
- Gap: PSD3 and PSR final vote. Why it matters: the application date (about 2028-11) drives EU build plans. Research question: Did the European Parliament adopt PSD3 and the PSR at second reading on 2026-12-14, and what is the Official Journal date?

Section 7.3
- Gap: Visa Compelling Evidence for condition 10.4 now accepts the agent provider's login ID. Why it matters: it is a concrete evidence hook for P9 that the table does not map. Research question: Can a merchant meet Visa Compelling Evidence for an Agentic Transaction using AP2 mandate or Trusted Agent Protocol data, and which fields must match?
- Gap: Visa 4.1.23.3 to 4.1.23.9 require consent, an instruction expiry date, identity verification and a 120 day order confirmation. Why it matters: protocol fit to these rules decides whether an AP2 or ACP flow is compliant on Visa. Research question: Do AP2 mandates and ACP delegated payment tokens carry the fields Visa rules 4.1.23.3 to 4.1.23.9 require?
- Gap: Amex claim process. Payments Dive says the buyer must send a purchase intent document showing the deviation. Research question: What are the claim terms, limits and exclusions of Amex Agent Purchase Protection?
