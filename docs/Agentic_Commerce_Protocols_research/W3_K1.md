## Gaps

Section 2

1. Which protocol carries Copilot Checkout and Meta's AI checkout today. Why it matters: it decides whether ACP is a live consumer protocol or a fading one, which changes the build to the token advice. Research question: Which protocol (ACP, UCP or proprietary) carries Copilot Checkout and Meta AI checkout payments in November 2026, per Microsoft, Meta, Stripe or PayPal primary pages?
2. Stale evidence behind Live and Pilot cells. Alipay, UPI, Adyen, PayPal, Worldpay and the Mastercard Europe pilot rest on sources older than 90 days. Why it matters: a client could pick a rail that has since grown or stopped. Research question: Is there a primary source dated after 2026-08-17 for Alipay AI Pay, UPI Reserve Pay agents, Adyen Agentic, PayPal and Worldpay agent checkout?
3. No volume for any card network programme. Visa and Mastercard both decline to give agentic volume. Why it matters: Live says nothing about scale, and only x402 and MPP have counts. Research question: Has Visa, Mastercard, Shopify, Google or Stripe disclosed agent initiated transaction counts or value for 2026?
4. Google Pay and Apple Pay are missing as agent credentials, although Google Pay is the credential in the only verified UCP Live case. Why it matters: the wallet may decide reach more than the protocol. Research question: What agent token or mandate does Google Pay use in Universal Cart, and can third party agents use Apple Pay?
5. Closed agent checkouts (Amazon Buy for Me and Rufus, Perplexity with PayPal, Meta Muse) are not in the landscape. Why it matters: large consumer volume may run on no open protocol at all. Research question: Which closed agent checkouts are live in November 2026, and what credential and protocol does each use?

Section 4 (completeness of P1 to P16 and V1 to V13, and the candidates)

1. Promote or drop. Promote P17 checkout location and P18 consent capture surface, since builders must pick both and they are not covered by P11 or P4. Promote V16 minimum ticket, since it gates the pay per call use case. Merge P20 settlement intermediary with V15 settlement mode into one parameter, "settlement path". Fold V14 into V2 as expiry per layer. Drop P19 governance to the decision criteria, since a builder does not set it. Drop P21 as a parameter and keep it as a constraint under P14, since the payer's bank sets it. Research question: For five shipping agent checkouts, which of P17, P18, P20 and V16 did the builder have to choose explicitly?
2. Refund and cancellation authority is missing. V7 lists "refunded" as an event, but nothing says who can trigger a refund, in what window, or how a final rail refunds. Why it matters: P7 and P9 are hollow without it. Research question: How do UCP, ACP, x402 and MPP specify refunds and cancellations, and who may start them?
3. Payment timing is missing (immediate, deferred, recurring, metered). AP2 agent recurrence, the MPP subscription intent and UPI Reserve Pay all exist. Why it matters: subscriptions and price watch buys carry different consent and liability. Research question: Which protocols support recurring or deferred agent payments, and what limits do network rules set?
4. The agent indicator sent to the issuer is missing (EMVCo Agentic Transaction Indicators, network agent flags). Why it matters: it decides how issuers score risk and who carries liability. Research question: What agent indicator fields do Visa, Mastercard and EMVCo define in the authorisation message, and are they mandatory?
5. Buyer data scope is missing: what personal data goes to the agent and the merchant, and what consent covers it. Why it matters: GDPR and India's data law apply even when the payment is fine. Research question: What buyer data does each protocol pass to the merchant and the agent, and what consent does it record?
