## Disputes settled

**x402.org 24.24M USD and 75.41M transactions.** The same pair is quoted, unchanged, by Forkast on 2026-07-10 and 2026-07-14, KuCoin citing Crypto Briefing on 2026-08-17 (rounded), Solana Compass on 2026-08-25 (citing x402.org), Crypto Briefing on 2026-09-15 ("prior 30 days", mid August) and Crypto Briefing on 2026-10-15 (citing x402.org). The reading therefore existed by 2026-07-10 and did not change for three months. Its 30 day window ends on or before 2026-07-10, so it covers roughly mid June to early July 2026. It is not an August figure and not an October figure. The tracker shows 4.8M (June) and 6.0M (July) for that period, so the dashboard count is about 12 to 16 times the tracker, not 4 times. The doc's pairing of 75.41M with the tracker's August 17.9M is wrong. Keep the tracker on the page (the doc's choice holds). If 24.24M stays anywhere, label it "x402.org dashboard, first quoted 2026-07-10, window not stated, vendor, rung 6". I could not open archived copies of x402.org to see the 2026-08-25 "page updated" date in C369 (Wayback refused the request).

**Mastercard 36 banks.** Rung 4 with the vendor tag. The footnote on Mastercard's 2026-06-02 page names 36 banks that "completed transactions", which is a deployment record, not a stated intent, and matches how the doc grades Google, AWS and Visa launches. Two fixes: Mastercard states no number (36 is our count of the footnote), and the page says "controlled, live" transactions, which is a pilot under section 6.1, not live. The data label "European banks with live agent payments" overstates.

**Checkout.com 3 percent.** Rung 6, not 7. The figure is on Checkout.com's own newsroom release dated 2026-06-09, attributed to "merchants across the UK and US". So the doc's "Not on Checkout.com's own page" is wrong (R5-11 opened the guide page, which does not carry it). It is a vendor survey of merchant estimates with no method on the page, which is rung 6 under the R5 grading rule. as_of should be 2026-06-09, not 2026-07-20 (the Forkast date).

## Gaps

1. **Who runs agenteconomy.to and what it filters.** The page's headline x402 numbers rest on one tracker whose operator and method page were not read. Research question: Who operates agenteconomy.to, and does its methodology exclude self dealing, wash and test transactions from x402 counts?
2. **The x402 trend is falling and the section does not say so plainly.** The tracker shows 17.9M in August, 13M in September and 5.1M in October, against a December 2025 peak of 54.2M. A client reading "200.7M lifetime" may assume growth. Research question: What were x402 monthly counts and settled values from January to November 2026 on two independent trackers, and was the August spike one facilitator?
3. **ACP and ChatGPT checkout status.** The section shows the ACP repo is quiet but not whether ChatGPT Instant Checkout still runs. A client choosing ACP needs that. Research question: Is ChatGPT Instant Checkout on ACP live for US merchants as of 2026-11, and has OpenAI published merchant or order counts?
4. **Mastercard Agent Pay after the controlled tests.** The 36 bank figure is a June pilot. Research question: Has Mastercard Agent Pay moved from controlled transactions to general availability for European or US cardholders by 2026-11, and with what volume?
5. **Method behind the merchant 3% estimate.** It is the only figure for agent share of all card transactions. Research question: What sample, markets and question wording sit behind Checkout.com's June 2026 finding that merchants say 3% of transactions involve AI agents?
