## Gaps

Sections 7.4 to 7.6

1. Gap: no field data on agent dispute or chargeback rates. Why it matters: every loss and dispute claim rests on lab, preprint and vendor work, so ADR-6 and the evidence store are sized without a real loss rate. Research question: Has any network, PSP or issuer published dispute, chargeback or refund rates for agent initiated transactions in Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay or ACP pilots by 2026-11?
2. Gap: first party misuse ("my agent did it" disputes). Why it matters: merchants may face more friendly fraud than hijack fraud, and the evidence record should be designed for it. Research question: Is there 2026 vendor or network data on consumers disputing purchases by claiming their agent erred, and how merchants won or lost them?
3. Gap: malicious MCP servers and agent skills as a purchase steering and credential theft path. Why it matters: R5-41 shows merchant files already push skill installs, and 7.6 treats only merchant content as untrusted. Research question: What 2026 papers or incidents show malicious MCP servers, plugins or agent skills steering purchases or taking payment data?
4. Gap: the traffic panel has no figure for agent completed orders. Why it matters: clients cannot size the gateway against real agent checkout volume. Research question: What share of US or EU ecommerce orders were completed by an AI agent (not a referral click) in 2026 per Adobe, Salesforce, Shopify or a network?
5. Gap: status of the remaining V12 x402 findings. Why it matters: 7.6 pins SDK versions but does not say which fixes exist. Research question: Has the x402 Foundation fixed or published advisories for V12's 22 non critical findings after 2026-10-25?

Section 10 (the ten are broadly the right ten, but these pains are missing or buried)

1. Gap: merchant loss of the customer relationship (data, loyalty, upsell, brand). Why it matters: it is a top merchant objection in most 2026 surveys and drives whether merchants open checkout to agents at all. Research question: In 2026 merchant surveys, what share name loss of customer relationship or data as a top barrier to agent checkout?
2. Gap: protocol fragmentation and integration cost (ACP, AP2, UCP, x402, MPP, TAP, Agent Pay). Why it matters: this is a protocols design doc, and "specs change under us" sits only in 10.4. Research question: How many agent protocols do Stripe, Adyen, Worldpay and Checkout.com support as of 2026-11, and what integration cost or time do merchants report?
3. Gap: returns, cancellations and post purchase servicing through an agent. Why it matters: refunds on final rails and order changes drive cost and disputes. Research question: What do merchants or PSPs report about return, cancellation or modification rates for agent placed orders in 2026?
4. Gap: privacy and data sharing as a ranked challenge. Why it matters: 10.4 shows it is the top concern in Europe (32 to 42 percent) yet it is not in the ten. Research question: Have the EDPB, ICO or FTC issued 2026 guidance on data sharing between shopping agents, platforms and merchants?
5. Gap: agent fees and commission economics. Why it matters: 80 percent of merchants expect a toll (R5-17), which affects margin and routing. Research question: What fees or commissions do ChatGPT Instant Checkout, Google UCP and Perplexity charge merchants per agent order as of 2026-11?

Ranking note: challenge 10 gets E 4 although 10.1 says its evidence is lab, preprint and vendor with no confirmed loss. With R5-61 removed (row 37) its non lab evidence thins further, so E 3 may be fairer.
