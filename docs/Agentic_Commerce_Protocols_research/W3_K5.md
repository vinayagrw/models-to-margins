## Tempo and the startup rule

- Founding test passes. Tempo was set up in 2025 (incubation reported August to September 2025).
- Ownership test is open. Stripe and Paradigm incubated it and "did not contribute capital" to the Series A. 500 million USD at about 5 billion USD buys roughly 10 percent, so about 90 percent sits with Stripe, Paradigm, founders and staff in unknown shares. Paradigm is a venture firm, not an incumbent, so the rule fails only if Stripe alone holds more than half. Paradigm's co-founder Matt Huang, who sits on Stripe's board, leads Tempo. "Provisional" is the fair label.
- If Tempo is excluded, Crossmint takes third on the same scores (Rain 255, Skyfire 240, Crossmint 180, Kite 160). Crossmint was founded in 2022 per search results and is venture backed, so it is eligible.

## Stripe, PayPal and Coinbase outside the top three

The cut line is Visa at 380 (340 with vendor reach cut).
- **Stripe 335.** Needs 45 more, for example L 3 to 4 and R 3 to 4. Under the vendor cut Stripe is 315 against 340, so one L point (25) ties it. Exclusion holds only narrowly. Its L 3 rests on vendor statements, and so do the L 4 scores for Mastercard (36 banks, vendor) and Google (vendor blog). The doc should either apply one standard or say why Stripe's evidence is weaker.
- **PayPal 210.** Needs 170 more. With P 1 its maximum is 325, so it cannot enter without authoring a protocol (P 4 or more). Exclusion is defensible.
- **Coinbase 210.** M 0 is wrong (row 32), and the corrected score is about 255. Needs 125 more, which would take P 5, L 4, R 4 and M 5 together. Exclusion is defensible.

2. Live score standard. Why it matters: the top three L 4 scores and Stripe's L 3 all rest on vendor sources, yet the criterion says "not a vendor claim". 3. Moves score consistency. Why it matters: Coinbase gets M 0 with two in-window moves, Tempo gets M 1 with two, Rain gets M 3 with one. Research question: What dated launches or partnerships did each scored candidate announce between 2026-08-17 and 2026-11-15?
