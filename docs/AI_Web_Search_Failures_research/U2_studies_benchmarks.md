# U2 Studies and benchmarks: how AI assistants fail when they search the web

Strand U2 (structured studies and benchmarks). Compiled 2026-10-25. Every number below comes from a page that was opened in this session unless the row says "not opened". Vendor self-reported numbers are tagged [VENDOR] and kept out of the headline findings.

Evidence ladder: 1 audited or reproduced, 2 primary record, 3 revealed behaviour, 4 deployment scale, 5 independent survey or credible secondary, 6 self-report, 7 vendor claim or unopened.

## Headline in five lines

1. Across 2,709 core answers in 14 languages, 45% of answers from ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini and Perplexity had at least one significant issue. Sourcing was the biggest cause at 31% (EBU and BBC, 2025-10-21).
2. In 1,600 citation tests across eight AI search tools, more than 60% of answers named the wrong article, publisher or URL. Rates ran from 37% (Perplexity) to 94% (Grok 3) (Tow Center, 2025-03-06).
3. When an AI search tool fails on same-day news, the cause is mostly retrieval. Over 70% of errors came from failing to land on, or diverging from, the right source (Suzgun et al., 2026-05-21).
4. Being correct is not the same as being grounded. Google AI Overviews were right about 91% of the time on SimpleQA, but only 39% were both right and fully backed by their cited pages (Oumi for the NYT, 2026-04-14, vendor-stake caveat).
5. Assistants almost never say "I don't know". Refusals fell to 0.5% of 3,113 questions (EBU 2025), and NewsGuard found chatbots answered 100% of prompts but repeated false claims 35% of the time (2025-09-04).
