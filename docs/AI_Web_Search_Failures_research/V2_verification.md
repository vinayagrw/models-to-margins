## 2. Claims marked NO or PARTLY

**VT6 (PARTLY)**: Seer: retrieved-only URLs rose from 9 to 34 in one capture  
Page says: "There were nine in the first capture and 34 in the second." (2026-10-22). The rise is across two captures 13 days apart (Sept 3 and Sept 16), not within one capture. The author also says retrieved-but-not-cited does not mean ignored, so it is weak evidence for snippet-only reading.  
Source: https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/chatgpt-data-structure

**VT9b (NO)**: Ahrefs shows most free answers never search  
Page says: "We originally said 14.4% of responses searched the web." (2026-10-12). Ahrefs corrected exactly this reading. 14.4% is the share with fan-out queries, not the share that searched. The page does not say the other 85.6% never searched.  
Source: https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt/

**C2.4 (PARTLY)**: GPT-6 Astra card 8.3.2 Broken Search Tool: GPT-5.6 Sol fails to admit 10 times as often

Source: https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra

**C5.3 (PARTLY)**: Pulse 2.0: Claude to insert Democracy Works info and live official results instead of relying on search or memory

Source: https://pulse2.com/anthropic-strengthens-claude-election-safeguards-ahead-of-2026-u-s-midterms/
