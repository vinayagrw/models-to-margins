## 2. Claims marked PARTLY or NO

- **V03 (partly)**: P2 causes: 2025 citation accuracy ranged 39.36% to 93.68%, top is Claude 3.7 Sonnet. Page says: "Claude-3.5-Sonnet w/Search ... 94.04". Top value is Claude 3.5 Sonnet at 94.04%, not 3.7 Sonnet at 93.68%. The scorecard E2 row has it right. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11763
- **V22 (partly)**: WashU: cited domains mean credibility 0.732 vs 0.645 for page-one results. Page says: "mean credibility is 0.732, compared with 0.611 across the 117,390 matched first-page URLs". Overall comparison is 0.611. The 0.645 figure is the Health category only. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14021v1
- **V26 (partly)**: NewsGuard Claude: state-affiliated citations rose from 4% to 15%. Page says: "Claude cited Russian state-affiliated sources only four percent of the time". Page gives 4% as a citation rate before. The 15% is the false-claim repeat rate, with state media cited each time. No 15% citation share is stated. Source: https://www.newsguardtech.com/special-reports/anthropic-ai-chatbot-claude-russia-iran-propaganda/
- **V59 (partly)**: Yu et al. (E2-3, P2): GPT-5-mini failed 75 of 100 XBench-DS tasks through premature stopping. Page says: "Premature Stop 12 41 1 75". 75 tasks show premature stopping, yet GPT-5-mini accuracy on that set is 41%. So not all 75 were failures. ICML 2026 FAGEN workshop confirmed. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29346v1
- **V71 (partly)**: Prefer study (E2-3, in window by HN date 2026-10-13): searched Perplexity 100%, ChatGPT 90%, Gemini 88.8%, Claude 80%, overlap 96/68/37/35. Page says: "Perplexity for 240 of 240, ChatGPT 216 (90%), Gemini 213 (88.8%)". All numbers match. Page is dated 19 September 2026 and data from 13 September, so it falls before the 30-day window. Source: https://tryprefer.com/data/ai-answer-study/
- **V86 (partly)**: U1 row 38: Google AI Overview said a hockey team had clinched a playoff spot. Page says: "can the Halifax Wanderers still make the CPL playoffs?". The team is a Canadian Premier League soccer club. No hockey mention in the thread. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49870367
- **V101 (partly)**: U2: Reuters DNR 2026, trust 20% overall and 44% among users, only 4% always or often click through vs 19% for search. Page says: "just 4% of respondents overall say they always or often click through". 4% is of all respondents in 27 markets, not of chatbot users. The page says the gap mostly reflects how few people use chatbots for news (9%). Source: https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/emerging-uses-ai-chatbots-news-and-what-it-means-journalism
