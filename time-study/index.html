Time study, 1826 to 2076

    Every tool that saves time gets spent on more.

    Faster travel bought longer trips. Appliances bought higher standards. Video calls bought more meetings. AI is the newest case, and people feel more rushed, not less.

    Answer in brief

    Two hundred years

    Paid hours roughly halved since 1870, yet weekly leisure grew only 4 to 5 hours. The rest went elsewhere.

    AI's role

    AI saves 15% to 55% on lab tasks but 2% to 6% of real work hours. Review absorbs the rest.

    AI is the newest time saver. Its ceiling is high, the measured saving is small, and checking and new tasks pull it back. Test the break-even below. Illustrative

    Details

    Net saved = G x s minus R minus p x F, for a 60 minute task.

    Inputs

    s is the share of the task the tool saves. R is extra demand that the saving pulls in: more trips, higher standards, more checking. p is the chance the work must be redone. F is the redo time.

    Presets

    Hours fixed keeps extra demand low. Full rebound lets demand eat the saving. AI outside the frontier is one example where checking and redo push it below zero.

    Status

    Illustrative. Presets are set to match the direction of measured cases, not measured values.

    Lab tasks show 15% to 55% speedups. Whole-job field studies show about 5.4% of hours saved for users, 1.4% to 1.6% of all hours. The gap is review time, uneven use and saved time going into other work.

    What this means for Ciklum (Estimate):

    1. Time-and-materials engineering and contact centre work deflate first. Pyramid revenue is the main risk.

    2. Move legacy work to fixed price and outcome contracts before clients force a 10% to 15% giveback at renewal.

    3. Sell agent build and run, evaluation, and managed AI operations as products.

    4. Price on cost per finished task, not cost per token. Budget review and rework hours in every bid.

    5. Junior hiring is where the first hit lands. Redesign the junior path rather than cut it.

    6. Offer clients a written split of saved hours (output, staff hours, quality). It answers the union and wellbeing question up front.

    Persona panel

    Ten experts, one finding: the saving is real but it is spent, unless hours are fixed.

    Panel conclusion. Tools save time on each task, but people, firms and markets spend most of it on more. AI will repeat this unless hours are fixed in writing.
