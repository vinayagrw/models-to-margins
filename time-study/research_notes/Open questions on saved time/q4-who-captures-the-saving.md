# When AI saves work time, who captures the saving: workers as shorter hours or pay, firms as output and margin, or customers as lower prices, and what does the labour share say?

Researched 2 November 2026. Scope: sources dated 2025 or 2026 that the study does not already hold. The repo already holds the BLS labour share series to Q2 2026, the IMF 2024 model shift of 5.5 points, the ILO and EPI productivity to pay gaps, Stansbury and Summers on worker power, and Humlum and Vestergaard 2025 (3 to 7 percent of gains passed to workers). None of those is repeated here.

## What is known

Workers: pay and hours
- Denmark, 2023 to 2025. Two years after ChatGPT launched, AI chatbot adoption had precise null effects on earnings and recorded hours at both the worker and the workplace level, ruling out effects larger than 2 percent. Adopters moved into higher paying occupations but too few to move average earnings. Verified (abstract). https://ideas.repec.org/p/crm/wpaper/26078.html
- Denmark, 2026. The same paper finds the main change is in how work is organised: employers reassign tasks and add new tasks in content generation, AI oversight and AI integration. Verified (abstract). https://ideas.repec.org/p/crm/wpaper/26078.html
- US and 11 other industrialised countries, data to 2019, paper January 2026. Autor and Kausik show the wage maximising labour share depends only on the capital to labour ratio, estimate the labour share is above that level in all 12 countries, so further automation should raise average wages, and find the falling labour share accounted for 16 percent of US real wage growth over 1954 to 2019. Verified (abstract). https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06343
- US model, 2026 to 2030, September 2026. In the Anthropic Institute scenarios the cognitive wage is 0.3 percent below its no AI path in 2030 in the substantial case and 11.5 percent below in the extreme case, while wages in all other occupations are 5.9 percent and 34 percent above it. Projection. https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/files/4zrzovbb/website/cf58f84d46a4a76bf5a5b039ac695fba6b80041c.pdf

Firms: output and margin (company attributions, treat as vendor claims)
- US, Q4 2025 call on 28 January 2026. C.H. Robinson reported average headcount down 12.9 percent year on year, operating margin excluding restructuring up 320 basis points, shipments per person per day up double digits in 2025 and more than 40 percent since 2022, 95 percent of missed LTL pickup checks automated by AI agents and about 350 hours of manual work saved a day. CFO Damon Lee: "This is the lean AI strategy at work." Vendor claim, Verified as a statement on the call. https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/01/28/ch-robinson-chrw-q4-2025-earnings-transcript
- US, Q4 2025 call on 17 February 2026. The Hackett Group booked 1 to 1.5 million dollars of Q1 2026 AI transition charges for severance as roles are replaced by GenAI platforms and named GenAI driven margin expansion as a 2026 lever. Vendor claim, Plausible (search summary, transcript not opened). https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/17/hackett-group-hckt-earnings-call-transcript
- US model, 2030, September 2026. In the Anthropic Institute extreme scenario the capital share rises from 40 to 55 percent of income while GDP ends about a third higher than without AI, so capital owners take the whole increase. The paper writes that "the labor share falls by a quarter while GDP rises by a third". Projection. https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/files/4zrzovbb/website/cf58f84d46a4a76bf5a5b039ac695fba6b80041c.pdf
- US model, 2030. Tabarrok's reading of the same paper: total labour income in 2030 in the extreme case is almost exactly what it would have been without AI (0.45 x 1.32 is about 0.60), so workers as a group keep their old income and get none of the gain. Plausible (commentary, 14 September 2026). https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2026/09/ai-redistribution-and-the-size-of-the-pie.html

Customers: prices
- US, 2026. The Minneapolis Fed notes that consumer and producer price indexes for software, historically deflationary, have been flat to rising since generative AI arrived. Plausible (search summary). https://www.minneapolisfed.org/article/2026/how-is-ai-influencing-interest-rates-investment-productivity-prices-and-more
- US, May 2026. Goldman Sachs (vendor view) expects AI to raise prices first through memory chips and electricity and to turn disinflationary only once gains are widespread. Consumer electricity prices were up 4.6 percent year on year with much of the rise attributed to data centre demand. Plausible (Fortune report). https://fortune.com/2026/05/04/is-ai-inflationary-goldman-sachs-gen-z/
- Global, 2026. State Street Global Advisors (vendor view) argues the disinflation case depends on how gains are allocated and expects partial and time varying pass through to prices. Plausible (commentary). https://www.ssga.com/us/en/institutional/insights/artificial-intelligence-productivity-disinflation

The labour share: latest data and dated projections
- US, Q3 2026. The BLS Productivity and Costs release for the third quarter of 2026 is scheduled for 5 November 2026, so on 2 November 2026 the latest labour share is still 52.8 percent for Q2 2026. Plausible (schedule from search summary). https://www.bls.gov/news.release/prod2.nr0.htm
- US, 2026 to 2030, September 2026. Anthropic Institute Working Paper 2026-02 (Korinek, Jones, Sacher, Cotter, McCrory, also CEPR DP 21939): labour share of income 60.0 percent today, 59.4 percent in 2030 under modest change, 56.1 percent under substantial change, 45.2 percent under extreme change. Projection, Verified from the paper text. https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/files/4zrzovbb/website/cf58f84d46a4a76bf5a5b039ac695fba6b80041c.pdf
- US, 2026 to 2030. The same paper says the substantial scenario shift of four points in four years is only slightly less than the entire fall in the US labour share over the four decades after 1980. Projection. https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:cpr:ceprdp:21939
- US, 2030. A median US adult surveyed in the same paper expects outcomes consistent with the substantial scenario: GDP up 8 percent and cognitive employment down 4 percent by 2030. Projection. https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:cpr:ceprdp:21939
- Long run, no date, October 2025. Restrepo (Yale), NBER Working Paper 34423: under AGI with expanding compute the labour share of GDP converges to zero and wages converge to the compute cost of reproducing human work. Projection (undated scenario). https://www.nber.org/papers/w34423
- Long run, April 2026 report of the same paper. Fortune reports the paper's conclusion that "Most income will accrue to owners of computing resources". Plausible (secondary). https://fortune.com/2026/04/04/ai-jobs-future-not-important-enough-to-be-automated-yale/
- Global, April 2026. IMF Notes 2026/002, from a December 2025 IMF scenario planning workshop, suggests fiscal analysis could include scenarios with a persistent decline in the labour income share. No number. Plausible (page returned 403, search summary only). https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/068/2026/002/article-A001-en.xml

Bargaining over the saved time
- US, 2023 to March 2026. AI Exposure (TheDataProject.ai) analysis of 47 collective agreements with AI provisions: advance notice 89 percent, retraining rights 76 percent, human review 72 percent, profit or savings sharing 23 percent (rated weak on enforceability), AI minimum staffing 15 percent, no clause on shorter hours. Plausible (single site, sample method not described). https://www.aiexposure.org/analysis/union-ai-bargaining
- US, June 2025 and October 2025. The same page lists a CWA deal with 30 day notice before AI driven layoffs and severance tied to AI savings (about 12,000 workers) and a UAW deal with a 250 million dollar AI and automation retraining fund over four years (146,000 workers). Plausible. https://www.aiexposure.org/analysis/union-ai-bargaining
- Australia, 4 June 2026. The AMWU and the ANMF campaign for a four day week, a 35 hour week or a nine day fortnight at full pay as the way to share AI gains, against a current 38 hour standard. A demand, no signed deal. Verified as a report of a demand. https://anz.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/employee-value-proposition/australian-unions-eye-three-day-week-as-ai-reshapes-work-50097
- US, March 2026. Labor Notes argues unions should claim AI gains as time and cites the Kickstarter 32 hour week at 100 percent pay as the precedent, which predates AI bargaining. Plausible (viewpoint). https://www.labornotes.org/blogs/2026/03/viewpoint-labors-answer-ai-give-us-our-time-back
- Germany, August 2026. IG Metall Baden-Wuerttemberg head Barbara Resch rejected an industry "productivity offensive" proposal of longer hours at unchanged pay. No 2026 agreement found that ties AI gains to hours. Plausible (news item partly AI generated by its own note). https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/tarifrunde-metall-ig-metall-lehnt-mehrarbeit-ohne-lohn-ab-935001

## Who is working on it

- Economic Scenarios for Transformative AI. Korinek, Jones, Sacher, Cotter and McCrory, The Anthropic Institute Working Paper 2026-02 and CEPR DP 21939, September 2026. A model that maps paths of AI capability to GDP, the labour share, wages, job reallocation and unemployment for 2026 to 2030, plus a survey of US adults. Latest finding: labour share of income 59.4, 56.1 or 45.2 percent in 2030 across modest, substantial and extreme scenarios, from 60.0 today, with cognitive wages 0.3 to 11.5 percent below their no AI path and other wages 5.9 to 34 percent above it. Horizon stops at 2030. https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/files/4zrzovbb/website/cf58f84d46a4a76bf5a5b039ac695fba6b80041c.pdf
- Still Waters, Rapid Currents: Early Labor Market Transformation under Generative AI. Humlum and Vestergaard, ROCKWOOL Foundation Berlin Discussion Paper 26078, 2026. Danish administrative earnings and hours linked to employer and worker adoption surveys, difference in differences. Latest finding: null on earnings and hours within 2 percent two years after ChatGPT, change shows up in task mix not pay. https://ideas.repec.org/p/crm/wpaper/26078.html
- Resolving the automation paradox: falling labor share, rising wages. Autor and Kausik, arXiv 2601.06343, January 2026. Theory plus data for 12 industrialised countries on whether a falling labour share lowers wages. Latest finding: labour share is above the wage maximising level in all 12, so automation that cuts the share can still raise wages, though the authors expect the shift to pose social and political challenges. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06343
- We Won't Be Missed: Work and Growth in the AGI World. Restrepo, NBER Working Paper 34423, October 2025. Long run growth model with bottleneck and supplementary work. Latest finding: labour share of GDP converges to zero, no date. https://www.nber.org/papers/w34423
- Global Economic and Financial Implications of Artificial Intelligence: Lessons from a Scenario Planning Exercise. IMF Notes 2026/002, April 2026. Synthesis of a December 2025 IMF workshop, qualitative scenarios for fiscal and financial analysis, names a persistent labour share decline as a scenario to model. No number yet. https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/068/2026/002/article-A001-en.xml
- BLS Productivity and Costs. Quarterly labour share for the nonfarm business sector, next release for Q3 2026 on 5 November 2026. https://www.bls.gov/news.release/prod2.nr0.htm
- Unions vs. AI: The New Collective Bargaining Frontier. AI Exposure (TheDataProject.ai), 5 March 2026. Tracker of AI clauses in 47 US collective agreements. Latest finding: 23 percent have profit or savings sharing, none has a shorter hours clause. https://www.aiexposure.org/analysis/union-ai-bargaining

Only commentary came back (State Street, Goldman Sachs via Fortune, Minneapolis Fed on software prices) and no study that measures pass through of an AI saving into a price. The design doc gap at line 722 stands.
- A labour share projection to 2040 or 2050 by a named institution. Not found. ## One line for a manager

So far the firm keeps the saving: Danish workers using AI chatbots saw no change in pay or hours two years on, US firms credit AI for lower headcount and higher margins on their own earnings calls, no study has yet measured a price cut for customers, and the only dated projection (Anthropic Institute, September 2026) has the US labour share falling from 60 percent to between 59 and 45 percent by 2030 depending on how fast AI advances, with no institution projecting it beyond 2030.
