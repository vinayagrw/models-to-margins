## (b) Marchetti radius: 2.5 km walking, 25 km rail, 50 km car within a 1 h budget
- Verdict: Plausible. Only the walking figure was confirmed.
- Source figure: Marchetti 1994, Technological Forecasting and Social Change 47:75-88. Walking at about 5 km/h gives "a territory radius of about 2.5 km". Mean travel time is about 1 h a day. Berlin grew from a 2.5 km radius (1800) to about 20 km with faster transport. No 25 km rail or 50 km car radius was found.
- URL: https://pure.iiasa.ac.at/id/eprint/4071/1/RR-95-04.pdf (TRID: https://trid.trb.org/View/716444)
- Suggested wording: "With about an hour a day for travel, a walking city spans about 2.5 km in radius. Faster transport stretched Berlin to about 20 km."

## (c) Share of waking life at paid work: 50% (1850s), 27.5% (1950s), 13.5% today
- Verdict: Plausible for 50%. 27.5% and 13.5% Not found.
- Source figure: Ausubel and Grübler 1995, TFSC 50(3):195-213. The share of disposable lifetime hours spent working fell from 50% (1856) to 20% (1981), UK. Lifetime hours worked fell from 124,000 to 69,000.
- URL: https://pure.iiasa.ac.at/4320 (PDF: https://pure.iiasa.ac.at/id/eprint/4461/1/RR-96-04.pdf)
- Suggested wording: "A British worker spent about half of his disposable lifetime hours at work in 1856, and about a fifth by 1981."
