## Safe to put on page

1. Working hours in France fell from about 3,170 a year in 1870 to about 1,540 in 2000.
2. Germany went from about 3,280 hours a year in 1870 to under 1,500 in 2000.
3. UK working hours fell by about 43 percent between 1870 and 2000.
4. South Korean hours rose to about 2,920 a year in 1970, then fell to about 1,900 by 2020.
5. American housewives spent about 52 hours a week on housework in the early 1900s and about 56 by the late 1960s, even as appliances spread.
6. Time spent on home laundry rose from about 5.5 to over 6 hours a week between 1925 and 1964.
7. Women's home work time fell only about 6 hours a week from 1900 to 1965, and most of the fall came later.
8. The IMF estimates that almost 40 percent of jobs worldwide are exposed to AI.
9. Daron Acemoglu estimates AI will raise total factor productivity by no more than 0.66 percent over ten years.
10. In a study of 5,179 support agents, AI lifted issues resolved per hour by 14 percent on average and 34 percent for novices.
11. A RAND workshop scenario for 2045 imagines two humanoid robots per person handling more than 95 percent of daily tasks.
12. One NBER study finds each 1 degree C of warming adds 2.5 deaths per 1,000 people a year.
