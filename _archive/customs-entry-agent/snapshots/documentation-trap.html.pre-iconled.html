One field is wrong, and nothing downstream can tell

A shipment does not usually fail because a ship was late. It fails because a line on a form was wrong before the ship sailed, and every checkpoint after that waved it through. Below, the same consignment runs twice: once the way it happens now, once with a check at the moment the line is written.
