## How this position differs from the common pitch

Industry messaging often leads with document extraction, product-code recommendations, and faster filing. Those capabilities are useful, but they are not the full safety design. A customs professional still has to own ambiguous classification, material exceptions, legal sign-off, and the evidence behind the declaration.

The proposed design therefore starts with repetition. It reuses a human-approved answer when the product, evidence, and current rulebook still agree. AI prepares the work that remains. A licensed specialist decides when the product is new, the evidence conflicts, or the financial and legal stakes are high.
