Five positions, not two

The axis below is sorted by one thing only: how much custody the customer holds. It is not sorted by capability, and it is not sorted by cost — a position further right is not smarter and not cheaper. Pick a position to see who actually holds the weights, the prompts, the logs and the legal boundary.

Custody chain

Frontier models now run air-gapped on customer hardware. Gemini ships via Google Distributed Cloud onto a single eight-GPU server with no link back to Google — it cannot be switched off remotely. Microsoft Foundry Local and M365 Local sit in the same position.

Open weights are available as managed APIs. Amazon Bedrock serves a managed open-weight catalogue in the mid-twenties of models; Vertex does the same. You can consume MIT- and Apache-licensed weights without ever touching a GPU.

So open vs closed and self-host vs managed are two independent axes, not one. The old binary — “public API or build it yourself” — describes only the two ends of a five-position line.
