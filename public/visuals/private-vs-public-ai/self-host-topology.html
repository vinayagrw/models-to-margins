Your cost structure, your numbers

Everything starts blank on purpose. We supply the arithmetic; you supply the figures.

We publish no break-even token volume and no GPU hourly rate. Every published break-even we could trace ran back to a vendor-adjacent source, and the largest single input — GPU $/hour — is directly contradicted across sources: 2026 figures disagree on whether rentals rose or fell, and quoted B200 rates span roughly a factor of two. A number built on that input would look precise and mean nothing.

Utilisation is the variable that decides this, and it is the one most often assumed rather than measured. Idle GPUs bill at full rate. A spiky workload loses on this trade regardless of the token price. If you cannot show a measured utilisation figure from the last thirty days, the calculator above is a hypothesis, not a business case.
