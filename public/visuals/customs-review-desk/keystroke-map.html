One keystroke, three records

Single-key model for the review desk. Click a key for what it writes when pressed.

Settings surface, required
  • Remap: every key above is rebindable in Reviewer Settings → Keyboard, no key is hard-coded to a physical position (A11Y-1).
  • Disable: single-key mode can be turned off entirely in favour of on-screen buttons plus Tab and Enter, for reviewers who cannot use rapid single-key input (A11Y-2).
  • Conflict check: remap screen blocks assigning the same key to two actions and warns on collision with screen-reader shortcuts.
