Seeded known-error probesOpen

Deliberate errors injected at a controlled, low prevalence into the routine lane, the only known way to keep detection alive once genuine errors go rare. Prevalence rate itself is a proposed 0.2% figure, not yet validated.

Agreement-rate drift monitorOpen

Tracks accept rate over time per reviewer and per field. A rising trend alone is not proof of vigilance loss, so this instrument flags for investigation, it does not score performance.

Structured uncertainty displayConfirmed

Banded risk name plus a subordinate number, never a bare score alone. Directly evidenced by the calibration research in Chapter 3, the one piece of this board resting on measured desk-level data.

Override-reason quality checkOpen

Free-text and templated override reasons are periodically sampled for whether they show actual reasoning versus a rubber-stamped label, since the reason field is a legal record, not telemetry alone.

What agreement rate alone must never beHold

A high accept rate is consistent with both a good model and a reviewer who stopped looking. None of the four instruments above is sufficient alone, and agreement rate by itself is never the success metric.
