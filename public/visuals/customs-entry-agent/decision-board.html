One control model, three different permissions

Pick the product situation. Every route answers the same four questions, so what changes between tabs is the permission line, not the process. Automate the repeatable preparation and keep judgment with customs professionals.

Proposed control model
The rule: the less the company knows about a product, the less the system is allowed to decide.

The same bracket has been approved before.

Proceed after validation
1 · The system recognises

The supplier, part number, description, origin evidence and approved product record all match.

2 · AI is allowed to

Compare the new documents with the approved record, surface differences and assemble the evidence packet.

3 · The human owns

The approval policy, audit samples and any difference that breaks the match.

4 · Outcome

Reuse the approved answer, validate it against the current rulebook, pass the gate and file. No new classification guess is needed.

It looks familiar, but one fact has changed.

Broker review required
1 · The system recognises

The line is close to a known product, but its material, use, origin, supplier or specification is different.

2 · AI is allowed to

Find the nearest approved decisions, identify the changed facts, retrieve relevant authorities and prepare a recommendation.

3 · The human owns

A customs broker reviews the difference, accepts or corrects the recommendation and records the reason.

4 · Outcome

Hold the line for broker review. The system prepares the case but does not turn similarity into certainty.

There is no approved answer to reuse.

Licensed decision required
1 · The system recognises

The product is genuinely new, the evidence conflicts, or the legal and financial stakes make automatic treatment unsafe.

2 · AI is allowed to

Organise source documents, retrieve candidate authorities, test arithmetic and show what remains uncertain.

3 · The human owns

A licensed specialist makes the classification and other material decisions, then signs off on the filing path.

4 · Outcome

No automatic filing. The new decision becomes a reusable record only after human approval and evidence capture.
