Fourteen choices, four of them load-bearing

A table makes all fourteen of these look equally weighted, and they are not. Four are architectural commitments the rest of the design rests on, marked here. The other ten are ordinary good practice. Open any tile for the alternative it rejected and what would flip it.

Design decisions, not measurements

Load-bearing means the rest of the design changes shape if the decision is reversed. The other ten would cost effort to swap and would not move the argument.
