Sourced

ATLAS draws contested rulings, where the code was already in dispute. The product benchmark draws rulings at random. That is why the same task looks 45 points different across the two, a sampling difference rather than a contradiction. Treat 40% as a contested-case floor, not a market-wide accuracy rate.

Why no 2026 model appears here All three are one-off papers rather than leaderboards anyone keeps up to date, and the newest closed scoring in October 2025. The only later measurement is a May 2026 workflow reaching 64.2% at six digits, which is a different depth and cannot be set against these ten-digit figures. No peer-reviewed ten-digit result has been published for any 2026-generation model.
