G1Prior disclosureCritical

When the watcher proves two years of misclassification, the tenant now has knowledge, and knowledge changes their legal position. A valid prior disclosure under 19 U.S.C. 1592(c)(4) can cut a gross-negligence penalty to interest. Historical findings must route to counsel on a privileged path, never into the ordinary review queue.

G4Bond sufficiencyHigh

A continuous bond is a standing financial guarantee, about 10% of duties paid in the prior 12 months, with a $50,000 floor. CBP reviews it monthly, and an insufficient bond holds cargo at the port. Tariff churn mechanically breaks bonds. The system already has the duty data, projecting bond breach is arithmetic, and it belongs in Wave 1.

G5Responsible supervision vs AIOpen

19 CFR 111.28 is written about people. Does a licensed broker approving 6,700 AI-drafted lines a month satisfy responsible supervision, or defeat it. No CBP guidance found. Ask the customer's counsel before Wave 3, not during it.
