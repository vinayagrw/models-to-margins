The four reproducibility pins ride on every row: model_id, prompt_version, corpus_version, rulebook_version. A null in any of these is a defect, not a missing nice-to-have. They are how a 2026 decision is re-derived in 2031 during a 19 U.S.C. 1592 proceeding.

Never update Rows are never updated. A change writes a new row with supersedes_id. Enforced by a database rule, not convention.
Reasonable care Reasonable care is a process standard. The defence is a record. Here is the documented basis, the ruling relied on, the licensed broker who approved it, the date, and the tariff version in force.
Retention Five years from date of entry (19 CFR 163.4). Proposed six years on S3 Object Lock compliance mode, which nobody can shorten, including the account root.
