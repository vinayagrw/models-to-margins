All window
The absence signal: Crunchtime ships no GA
No GA announcement for AI Analyst, AI Actions, or Photo Intelligence in May or June, roughly two months after the early-access launch. Against PAR's in-window production numbers, the April Crunchtime-vs-PAR read inverts.
The caution

Gartner's agent-washing anchor still stands: only ~130 of thousands of agentic vendors judged real, and over 40% of agentic projects predicted canceled by end-2027. The narrative swung fully to execution in this window, but verified production evidence remains thin. Evidence-before-autonomy is the defensible position.
