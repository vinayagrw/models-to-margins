Five forces in, one shape out

The extraction recipe: answer for your system

The gates above place one capability. This places your whole system. Answers are preset to a payments-grade platform; flip them to yours.

The token walks the five forces. Flip an answer to change the verdict.
Force 1 · Consistency
Do capabilities share one transaction boundary — a ledger that can never be a saga?
Sagas cannot give atomicity. Splitting the ledger is where migrations actually fail.
Force 2 · Latency
Is your synchronous hot path on a tail-latency budget where every hop costs p99?
Five sequential 99.99% hops compound to 99.95%. The hop tax is a tail, not a mean.
Force 3 · Compliance
Do you hold regulated data whose audit scope follows access — card data, PHI?
A mesh inside the compliance boundary multiplies audit evidence. One enclave, tokens elsewhere.
Force 4 · Cadence
Does one capability have a divergent cadence, stack, or scaling profile — and a durable owning team?
Both halves required. A seam without an owner is an outage looking for a pager.
Force 5 · Coordination
Are hundreds of engineers queuing on one release train — the release-coordination-manager tell?
The day that job is posted, coordination cost has become the dominant force.
