The counterweightsthe checks that keep the wall honest
Not worse

Greptile telemetry across 65,000 orgs: AI PRs are 27.6 percent of merges. Codex reverts 1.19 per 1,000 vs a human baseline of 2.72. Two of four agents beat humans.

Durability down

GitClear, 211M changed lines: churn doubled from 3.3 to 7.1 percent as AI adoption rose. Throughput up, rework up.

Grader gap

LangChain survey, 1,340 respondents: 89 percent have observability, only 37.3 percent run online evals. Most loops fly ungraded.

Review ghosting

MSR 2026 study of 33,707 agent PRs: 28.3 percent merge almost instantly, but agents abandon PRs when iterative human review pushes back.
